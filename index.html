Quick User Guide

1
Load Images: Drag and drop a single image or multiple sequence files into the window, or click Open File. Numbered frames sort automatically.
2
Calibrate Lens FOV: Adjust Source FOV in the sidebar (typically 160°–180° for doorbells), or click Add 3-Point Line to trace curved real-world straight lines (doorframes, walls) and click Optimize Source FOV.
3
Frame the Shot: Adjust Virtual Zoom, Tilt, Pitch, and Roll, or drag directly on the canvas to reorient the camera.
4
Export Results: Download the current frame with Export Image (PNG), or batch-export all sequence frames with Export Image Sequence. Save reusable camera settings with Export Settings.
